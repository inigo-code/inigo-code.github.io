Questions

Are they really free?

Yes. Both apps are free, with no ads, no subscriptions and no paid extras, and that won’t change.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one. An account only adds sync, so your prompts or your diary follow you to another phone.

Where is my data kept?

On your phone first. If you sign in, a copy is kept in your own private account, which only you can read. Each app’s privacy policy lists exactly what is kept: Prompt Heart, Watch Heart.

How do I install them?

Add the app’s F-Droid repository once and updates arrive like any other app. Or download the APK from GitHub and open it on your phone.

Are they on Google Play or iPhone?

Not yet on Google Play; it’s next. They’re Android apps, so there is no iPhone version.

How do I delete my account?

In either app, open Settings and choose Delete account. Each app’s website also explains how to ask for deletion without the app.
